# Proposal: Dashboard Market Position

Date: 2026-05-28  
Status: archive summary

## Insight
Generic BI dashboards show metrics. This product should produce newsroom decisions.

## Proposal
- Position the app as editorial intelligence, not generic analytics.
- Prioritize briefs, caveats, freshness, and workflow actions.
- Reduce chart overload.
- Make copyable evidence a first-class feature.

## Risks
- Too many metrics dilute editorial value.
- BI-style UI may feel passive.

## Acceptance
- Every major widget answers: what changed, why it matters, what to do next.
