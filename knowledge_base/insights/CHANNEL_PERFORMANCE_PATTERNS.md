## Key Findings

### Finding 1: Time-of-Day Effects
- **Observation:** Videos published 08:00–10:00 UTC receive 35–40% more views in first 24h
- **Channels Affected:** All (UATV English, French, Hungarian)
- **Recommended Action:** Schedule breaking news for morning slots
- **Confidence:** HIGH (n=1,000+ videos)
- **Caveat:** Effect weakens after 72h (views plateau)

---

### Finding 2: Weekend Viewership Drops
- **Observation:** Videos published Saturday–Sunday receive 20% fewer views (first 24h)
- **Recovery:** Full recovery by Wednesday
- **Channels Affected:** All
- **Recommended Action:** Publish critical stories on weekdays
- **Confidence:** MEDIUM (seasonal variation not accounted for)

---

### Finding 3: Viral Keyword Correlation
- **Keywords that correlate with 3x+ view uplift:**
  - "Breaking News" (+280% avg. views)
  - "Exclusive" (+150% avg. views)
  - "Live Update" (+120% avg. views)
- **Channels Affected:** Especially UATV English
- **Caveat:** Correlation ≠ Causation (could be news importance, not keyword)

## Recommendations for Editorial Team

1. **Publish breaking news at 08:00 UTC** (optimal reach)
2. **Avoid weekend publishing** (unless truly critical)
3. **Use proven keywords** (Breaking, Exclusive, Update) in titles
4. **Track view velocity first 4 hours** (predicts final performance)
5. **Engage viewers early** (first comment matters — engagement >2%)
